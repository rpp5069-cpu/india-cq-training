India Cultural Intelligence Training

ENGR 802: Engineering Across Cultures · Penn State World Campus · ~60–90 minutes

This self-paced training prepares U.S.-trained engineers to work effectively with engineering teams in India. You will build Cultural Intelligence (CQ) across four capabilities — Drive, Knowledge, Strategy, and Action — through interactive modules, branching scenarios, knowledge checks, and personal reflections.

The Four CQ Capabilities

Cultural Intelligence (CQ) is the ability to work effectively across cultural contexts. It has four measurable capabilities:

CQ Drive

Motivation

Your interest, confidence, and drive to adapt in culturally diverse situations. High CQ Drive means you lean into discomfort rather than avoiding it.

CQ Knowledge

Understanding

Your understanding of how cultures differ across values, communication styles, and social norms. For India: Hofstede dimensions, GLOBE leadership, and regional diversity.

CQ Strategy

Planning

Your ability to plan for and make sense of cross-cultural interactions. High CQ Strategy means you notice cultural cues and adjust your mental models in real time.

CQ Action

Adaptation

Your ability to adapt verbal and non-verbal behavior when needed. High CQ Action means you can flex your communication style without losing authenticity.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this training, you will be able to:

  • Drive Articulate why cultural preparation matters before engaging with India-based engineering partners, and identify your own CQ motivation gaps.
  • Knowledge Explain India's Hofstede cultural profile and GLOBE leadership data and describe their implications for day-to-day engineering collaboration.
  • Strategy Apply a pre-interaction planning checklist for high-PDI, collectivist team contexts, including private briefings, face-saving communication, and boundary-object creation.
  • Action Select specific do/avoid behaviors for meetings, feedback, hierarchy, and motivation — and build a 30-day action plan for your first India-based engagement.

How This Training Works

Self-paced, browser-based. All progress, quiz answers, and reflections are saved automatically to this browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Use the Download button at the end to save your completion record as a printable file.

  • CQ Pre-Check — Rate yourself on 8 items before the training begins. Your scores are saved and compared at the end.
  • Modules 1–7 — Each module has a scenario, knowledge section, strategy checklist, do/avoid table, branching scenario, knowledge checks, and a reflection prompt.
  • Capstone — Respond to three peer questions from your team's presentation Q&A. Compare your answers to model responses.
  • 30-Day Action Plan — Write one commitment per CQ capability. Saved to your completion record.
  • CQ Post-Check — Re-rate yourself on the same 8 items and download your completion record.
