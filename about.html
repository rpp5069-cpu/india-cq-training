About This Training

ENGR 802: Engineering Across Cultures · Penn State World Campus · Dr. Esther Obonyo · November 2026

Course Context

This training site was developed as the ENGR 802 final project by a five-member team at Penn State World Campus. The course examines how Cultural Intelligence (CQ) enables engineers to work effectively across national and cultural boundaries. The training is designed for U.S.-trained engineers preparing to work with Indian engineering teams.

The site translates the team's presentation research into a self-paced, interactive learning experience. All module content is drawn directly from the team's slide deck ("Team India Final Project," November 12, 2026) and the accompanying evidence organizer, with citations frozen to the deck reference list (slides 57–62) per course documentation requirements.

Technical Notes

This site is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript application with no server or login. Progress, quiz answers, and reflections are saved to your browser's localStorage under the key india-cq-v1. No data leaves your browser. The site can be deployed to GitHub Pages from the index.html root.

  • Typography: Outfit (headings) + Inter (body) via Google Fonts
  • Charts: Chart.js 4.4.4 (Hofstede bar, UPI bar); inline SVG (Rogers bell curve)
  • Icons: Lucide 0.447.0
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA — skip links, focus-visible, semantic headings, ARIA roles, keyboard navigation
  • Color palette: blue #0072B2 (do/enablers), orange #D55E00 (avoid/risks) — color-blind safe
  • Dark mode: system preference auto-detect + manual toggle
